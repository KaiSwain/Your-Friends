Support for Your Friends App

Have a question, found a bug, or need help with your account? Send us a note and we will do our best to help.

Contact support

The best way to reach us is by email. Please include the email address on your account, the device you are using, and a short description of what you need help with.

Email support

Common reasons to reach out

  • Help signing in or accessing your account.
  • Questions about privacy, data, or deleting your account.
  • Reporting a bug or something that does not look right.
  • Sharing feedback about how the app could feel more helpful.

Support email
